Step by Step

How the Smart Bag Assistant Works

1
RFID Tags on Every Book
RFID key-fob tags are attached to each book or notebook — one per subject.
2
Scan as You Pack
As the child places each book in the bag, the RFID reader scans the tag automatically.
3
LED Turns Green
Each subject's LED on the display panel changes from red to green — giving instant visual confirmation that the book is in the bag.
4
Child Confirms All 5 Are Green
The child can see at a glance whether all five subjects are packed. No forgotten books, no morning panic.
✓
All 5 LEDs Green — Ready for School!
The child leaves for school confidently, knowing every book is packed. Reduces morning anxiety for both child and parent.
Components

Hardware Used

RFID RFID reader module + RFID key-fob tags (one per book/notebook)
MCU Arduino Uno — processes RFID scan events and controls the LED panel
LEDs 5 dual-colour LEDs (red/green) — one per subject on the display panel
Power USB or battery-powered — portable and reusable every school day
Impact

Why It Matters

Independence

Child packs independently without a parent checking every book.

Less Anxiety

Eliminates the morning panic of forgotten books and rushed checking.

Visual Confirmation

Clear red/green feedback — no reading required, works for all ages.

Affordable

Built with low-cost components — reusable across all school years.
