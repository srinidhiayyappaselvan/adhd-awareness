Focus Smartwatch
Wearable Focus Reminder for Children with ADHD

A lightweight wristband with haptic vibrations and an OLED "FOCUS" reminder — silent, non-disruptive, and effective in any classroom.

Custom Microcontroller Haptic Motor OLED Display LiPo Battery
How It Works

Gentle Reminders. Zero Disruption.

During study sessions the watch delivers gentle vibration reminders at programmed intervals and displays FOCUS on its OLED screen — helping the child refocus without disturbing others in the classroom.

⌚
  1. Child wears the watch on their wrist during a study or class session.
  2. At programmed intervals (e.g. every 10 minutes), the haptic motor delivers a gentle buzz.
  3. The OLED screen displays "FOCUS" — a silent visual cue to refocus.
  4. The child self-corrects without any teacher intervention or social embarrassment.
  5. The watch runs on a rechargeable LiPo battery — light enough to wear all day.

Haptic Vibration

Gentle reminders at set intervals — silent to everyone else in the room.

OLED Display

Displays "FOCUS" and a countdown timer — a clear, private visual cue.

LiPo Battery

Rechargeable — lightweight enough to wear throughout the entire school day.

Non-Disruptive

Silent operation — no sound, no flashing lights. Only the wearer knows.

Components

Technical Specifications

MicrocontrollerCustom low-power microcontroller (ATtiny / ESP32-based wristband board)
DisplayOLED screen (0.96") — shows FOCUS message and interval countdown
Haptic MotorMiniature eccentric rotating mass (ERM) vibration motor
BatteryRechargeable LiPo (Lithium Polymer) battery — USB-C recharge
Reminder IntervalProgrammable — typically 10–15 minutes during study sessions
Form FactorWristband — lightweight, wearable all day, child-friendly design
Sound OutputNone — fully silent operation for classroom use
Impact

Why This Helps Children with ADHD

Combats Time Blindness

Children with ADHD often lose track of time entirely. Regular haptic cues restore their sense of time passing during study sessions.

Preserves Dignity

The reminder is private — no teacher calling the child out, no embarrassment. The child self-regulates independently and discreetly.

Builds Self-Regulation

Over time, the external cue trains the child's internal attention system — building the habit of self-checking and refocusing independently.
